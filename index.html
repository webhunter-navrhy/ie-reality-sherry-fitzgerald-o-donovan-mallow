About the property

Description property

An excellent opportunity to acquire a well-proportioned three-bedroom detached bungalow, ideally suited to first-time buyers, downsizers or those looking for a comfortable home within easy reach of the town centre.

The property occupies a generous site with a tarmacadam driveway providing ample off-street parking, enclosed by a wall boundary. The mature front garden provides an attractive approach to the property, while the rear grounds are designed with ease of maintenance in mind.

This property may be eligible for the Vacant Property Refurbishment Grant, subject to the relevant qualifying criteria, which could provide a significant opportunity for the successful purchaser to transform the property into their ideal home.

A viewing comes highly recommended and by appointment only with sole selling agents Sherry FitzGerald O Donovan.
